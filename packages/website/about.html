About QuickPOS

Retail software built around clear daily store work.

QuickPOS brings checkout, inventory, customers, staff access, receipts, and reports into one installed point-of-sale system for growing stores.

What we make

One operational record from checkout to owner report.

QuickPOS is designed to reduce the gaps between what happens at the counter and what an owner sees later. A completed order can update stock, retain the payment method and cashier record, appear in sales reports, and support a printable receipt without rebuilding the same transaction in separate tools.

Store owners create and activate their account on this website, then use the protected portal to download the official app for their devices.

SellCheckout, barcode scanning, customer selection, payment records, and receipts.
ControlProducts, stock adjustments, suppliers, staff roles, and store settings.
UnderstandRevenue summaries, order history, reports, statements, and exports.
Product principles

Clear access, verified payments, and practical support.

The product and website are structured so store owners can understand what they are buying, how access is activated, and where to get help.

01 · COMPLETE TOOLKIT

Core retail features stay together.

Every active store plan includes the operational modules published on the QuickPOS features page rather than splitting essential tools across hidden feature tiers.

  • Sales, inventory, customers, and orders
  • Staff roles and store settings
  • Reports, exports, and statements
  • Updates and product support
02 · VERIFIED ACCESS

Payment confirmation controls activation.

Subscription payments are completed through supported hosted payment providers. QuickPOS verifies the provider transaction before activating paid store access.

  • Paystack and Flutterwave checkout options
  • NGN pricing for customers in Nigeria
  • USD pricing for customers outside Nigeria
  • No card number storage on QuickPOS servers
03 · INSTALLED APP

Use the software on supported store devices.

QuickPOS is distributed as an installed application. Active owners use their protected account portal to obtain the current official build for each supported platform.

  • Windows desktop installer
  • Android app package
  • macOS builds for Intel and Apple silicon
  • Linux AppImage and Debian packages
04 · HUMAN SUPPORT

A published route for questions and problems.

Customers can contact the support team through the website support form or by email for account, installation, billing, and product assistance.

  • Support request form
  • Email support at support@quickpos.com.ng
  • Installation and device guidance
  • Billing and account assistance
