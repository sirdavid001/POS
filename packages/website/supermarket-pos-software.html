Frequently asked questions

Supermarket POS questions answered.

Is QuickPOS suitable for a supermarket in Nigeria?

Yes. QuickPOS is built for retail counters and supports barcode checkout, inventory with low-stock thresholds, separate cashier accounts, receipts, and sales reports. Stores in Nigeria pay in naira.

Can I use a barcode scanner with QuickPOS?

Yes. QuickPOS works with USB and Bluetooth barcode scanners that type the scanned code like a keyboard, and it can also scan barcodes with a device camera.

How many cashiers can use one store account?

Each QuickPOS store subscription includes unlimited staff accounts for that store, with admin, manager, and cashier roles.

Does QuickPOS work when the internet goes off?

After the installed app has synced online, supported sales can be recorded during a connection interruption and synchronized when the connection returns. Actions such as subscription payments and password resets still need the internet.

Can QuickPOS sell items by weight?

QuickPOS records sales in whole units. Supermarkets that sell loose goods such as rice, beans, or garri usually pre-pack them into fixed sizes and give each size its own price and barcode.

Does QuickPOS track expiry dates?

QuickPOS does not currently track batch numbers or expiry dates. Stores that need close expiry control should keep that check in their receiving process alongside QuickPOS stock records.
