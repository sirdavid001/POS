Pricing questions

Understand the full store cost before activating.

How much does it cost to start using QuickPOS?

A new store starts with a one-time activation payment of ₦20,000 / $15. The activation includes five months of full store access.

What happens after the five-month activation period?

After activation, the store can choose a monthly, quarterly, or yearly renewal. Every renewal includes the same QuickPOS features.

Does QuickPOS charge separately for staff or devices?

No. One active store subscription includes unlimited staff accounts and supported devices for that store. QuickPOS does not charge a separate per-device fee.

Which currency will I pay in?

Customers in Nigeria pay in Nigerian naira. Customers outside Nigeria pay in US dollars. QuickPOS checks the billing location again before creating provider checkout.

What happens when a QuickPOS renewal is cancelled?

Cancelling stops future automatic renewal. The store keeps access through the paid period, subject to the billing terms and refund policy.
