Questions from retail owners

Inventory and sales software FAQ.

Clear answers about what QuickPOS records, how stock updates, and what your store needs to get started.

Does QuickPOS update inventory after every sale?

Yes. When a completed sale reaches QuickPOS, the sold quantities are deducted from the store's inventory records. Owners and authorized staff can also record stock-in, stock-out, and correction adjustments with reason notes.

Can QuickPOS keep recording sales when the internet drops?

The installed QuickPOS app can queue sales locally when the connection drops and synchronize them when connectivity returns. Offline access is subject to the store's last verified entitlement and known paid expiry date. See the complete offline POS software guide.

Which payment methods can a store record in QuickPOS?

At checkout, staff can record the payment method used for a sale, including cash, card, transfer, and supported mobile payment records. QuickPOS records the retail sale; a separate card terminal or payment service may be used to collect the customer's payment.

Which devices support QuickPOS?

QuickPOS provides installed-app packages for Windows, Android, macOS, and compatible 64-bit Linux systems. Store owners access the current official installers through the protected account portal.

Do staff members need separate QuickPOS subscriptions?

No. One active store subscription covers the staff accounts and supported devices belonging to that store. Owners can assign admin, manager, and cashier roles to control access.
