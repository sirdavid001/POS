Frequently asked questions

What store owners ask about QuickPOS reports.

Which sales reports are available in QuickPOS?

QuickPOS provides revenue summaries, sales trends, recent orders, top-product views, order details, and store statements. Owners can use date filters to review different reporting periods.

Can I see which cashier recorded a sale?

Yes. Completed QuickPOS orders retain the staff attribution available for the sale, alongside its items, customer, totals, payment method, and timestamps.

Can QuickPOS reports be downloaded?

Yes. Depending on the record or statement, QuickPOS supports CSV, PDF, Excel, print, or email delivery options.

Can reports separate cash, card, and transfer sales?

QuickPOS keeps the recorded payment method with each completed order, allowing owners to review whether a sale was recorded as cash, card, transfer, or another configured method.

Are reports available during an internet interruption?

The installed app can derive supported reports from its most recent local store snapshot. Server-fresh records and emailed statement delivery require an internet connection.
