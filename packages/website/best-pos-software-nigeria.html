The checklist

Nine questions to ask before you pay for POS software.

Ask every vendor the same questions and write down the answers. A short demo or trial on your own products will tell you more than any brochure.

01

Does it keep working when the network drops?

Network and power interruptions happen. Ask which actions work offline, how long offline use lasts, and how queued sales are synchronized afterwards.

02

Is it software, a payment terminal, or both?

Many people in Nigeria say “POS” for the bank terminal. Confirm whether the product records items, stock, and receipts, or only processes payments.

03

Does it support barcode scanning?

Check camera, USB, and Bluetooth scanner support, and whether products can still be found by name or SKU when a label is missing.

04

Does stock update with every sale?

Inventory should reduce automatically when a sale completes, with low-stock alerts and a history of adjustments that helps you trace missing stock.

05

Can each staff member have their own login?

Separate cashier, manager, and owner access protects prices, stock, and reports, and links every sale to the person who recorded it.

06

What reports can the owner see?

Look for revenue, order counts, discounts, tax, top products, and payment methods, plus PDF, Excel, or CSV exports for your accountant.

07

Which devices does it run on?

Confirm support for devices you already own, such as a Windows laptop, a Mac, or an Android tablet, before buying new hardware.

08

What is the full cost in naira?

Ask about setup fees, renewal prices, extra-user charges, and what happens when a renewal is late. Compare the total over a year, not only the first month.

09

Who supports you, and how?

Find out how to reach support, how quickly they respond, and whether the vendor is a registered business you can identify.

There is no single best POS software for every store. The right choice depends on whether you need offline selling, barcode scanning, inventory control, staff accounts, reports, and pricing that fits your budget. Use the checklist on this page to compare options, including QuickPOS.

What is the difference between a POS machine and POS software?

A POS machine is a payment terminal that processes card or transfer payments. POS software records what was sold and connects each sale to products, stock, staff, receipts, and reports. Many stores use both together.

Can POS software work without internet in Nigeria?

Some can. QuickPOS lets supported sales continue during a connection interruption after the installed app has synced online, then synchronizes the queued sales when the connection returns.
