Practical POS guide for Nigeria

A POS machine takes payment. POS software runs the sale.

In Nigeria, “POS” often means the bank or payment terminal used for card and transfer transactions. Retail POS software does a different job: it records what was sold and connects checkout to products, stock, staff, customers, receipts, orders, and reports.

The short answer

A payment record is not the same as a sales record.

If a terminal confirms that ₦25,000 moved, the store still needs to know which products made up that amount, how many units left the shelf, who served the customer, whether tax or a discount applied, and where to find the receipt later.

A POS machine or payment terminal

  • Connects to a bank or payment provider
  • Processes or confirms a financial transaction
  • Shows the amount and payment status
  • May print a payment-provider receipt
  • Does not automatically know the products sold

Retail POS software

  • Builds the cart from saved products
  • Records quantities, tax, discounts, and total
  • Associates the cashier and customer
  • Updates recorded inventory
  • Creates order history and store reports

Used together

  • Prepare the order in the POS software
  • Take payment through the chosen provider
  • Confirm the payment result
  • Complete the order with the method recorded
  • Keep both payment and retail records
Example at the counter

How the two systems can work during one sale.

01

Build the cart

The cashier searches or scans the customer's products in QuickPOS, checks quantities, selects the customer if needed, and confirms tax, discount, and total.

02

Take the payment

For card or provider transfer, the cashier uses the store's chosen terminal or bank account. For cash, the cashier receives and checks the amount directly.

03

Complete the retail record

After confirming payment, the cashier records the method in QuickPOS and completes the sale. The order, receipt, stock deduction, staff record, and reporting context remain connected.

Frequently asked questions

POS machine and POS software questions answered.

Is a POS machine the same as POS software?

No. A POS machine commonly means a bank or payment terminal that processes a card or transfer transaction. Retail POS software records what the customer bought and connects the sale to products, stock, staff, customers, receipts, and reports.

Do I still need a payment terminal if I use QuickPOS?

A store that accepts card payments will still need its chosen bank or payment provider terminal. QuickPOS records the retail sale and its payment method but does not claim to replace that terminal.

Can QuickPOS record cash and transfer sales?

Yes. QuickPOS can record cash, card, transfer, or other configured sale methods so the completed order keeps the method used by the store.

Why use POS software if the terminal already shows transactions?

A terminal transaction normally confirms an amount and payment status. POS software keeps the business context: products, quantities, prices, tax, discount, cashier, customer, stock deduction, receipt, and reporting.

Can a retail store use a POS machine and QuickPOS together?

Yes. The cashier can prepare the sale in QuickPOS, take payment through the store's chosen terminal or account, and complete the QuickPOS order with card or transfer recorded as the payment method.
