A connected product record

The barcode should open the right item, not a separate spreadsheet.

Each QuickPOS product can keep the details the store needs at checkout and afterward: name, SKU, barcode, category, selling price, cost price, stock quantity, low-stock threshold, and product image.

01

Create a clean catalog

Save the barcode with the product instead of maintaining a separate lookup list. The same product record supports search, checkout, stock control, order history, and reporting.

02

Choose the scanning method

Use an available device camera or a compatible USB or Bluetooth scanner. Hardware scanners that act like keyboards can enter the code directly into the focused QuickPOS field.

03

Keep search as a fallback

If packaging is damaged or a code has not been saved, the cashier can search the catalog instead of blocking the queue. An authorized user can correct the record for the next sale.

04

Connect the scan to stock

The scan finds the product for the cart. Completing the sale records the items, quantities, cashier, payment method, customer, totals, and stock deduction as one order workflow.

Scanner choices

Use the input method that fits the counter.

QuickPOS supports several practical scanning paths, but it does not promise that every camera or scanner model is compatible. Test the store's chosen hardware and keep catalog search ready as the fallback.

01

Device camera

Grant camera permission on a compatible Windows, Android, or macOS device and open the scanner from the supported product or checkout screen.

02

USB scanner

Connect a scanner that behaves like a keyboard, focus the QuickPOS barcode field, and scan. The device sends the code as typed input.

03

Bluetooth scanner

Pair a compatible scanner with the computer, tablet, or phone, confirm that it enters text correctly, and then test it with saved QuickPOS products.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about barcode scanning with QuickPOS.

Can QuickPOS scan product barcodes?

Yes. QuickPOS stores a barcode with each product and can use a compatible device camera or a scanner that enters barcode data like a keyboard.

Can I use a USB barcode scanner with QuickPOS?

Many USB barcode scanners behave like keyboards and can enter a scanned code into QuickPOS. Compatibility depends on the scanner, operating system, and device configuration, so stores should test their chosen model.

Does QuickPOS support camera barcode scanning?

Yes. On a compatible device, the store can grant QuickPOS camera permission and use the product or checkout scanner. Camera availability depends on the device and operating system permissions.

What happens when a scanned barcode is not found?

The cashier can search the product catalog by product details, while an authorized user can add or correct the product barcode for future checkout.

Does a barcode sale update inventory?

Yes. Barcode scanning finds the saved product for the cart. When the sale is completed, QuickPOS records the order and deducts the sold quantities from recorded stock.
