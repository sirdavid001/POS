From shelf to sales report

A point of sale system should connect the whole retail cycle.

When checkout and inventory live in separate records, store teams spend time correcting quantities and owners struggle to understand the day. QuickPOS keeps the product catalog, completed orders, stock movements, customer details, staff activity, and reports in one store workspace.

01

Build a searchable product catalog

Add product names, SKUs, barcodes, categories, images, selling prices, cost prices, stock quantities, and low-stock thresholds. Cashiers can search the catalog or scan a saved barcode at checkout.

02

Complete a clear checkout

Control cart quantities, apply the store's tax and discounts, select a customer, record the payment method, and preview or print the receipt. QuickPOS keeps the cashier, items, totals, and payment record attached to the order.

03

Keep inventory accountable

Completed sales reduce recorded stock automatically. Staff can also log stock-in, stock-out, and correction adjustments with a reason, while owners retain an inventory history with user attribution.

04

Turn transactions into useful reports

Review revenue cards, sales trends, recent orders, and top products. Use date filters for daily, weekly, and monthly analysis, then export the records or prepare a store statement.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about retail POS software.

What is retail POS software?

Retail POS software records sales at checkout and connects them to products, stock quantities, receipts, customer records, staff activity, orders, and business reports.

Does QuickPOS support barcode scanning?

Yes. QuickPOS supports product barcode records and scanning with compatible camera, USB, and Bluetooth scanning options. A cashier can also search the product catalog when a barcode is unavailable.

Does QuickPOS update inventory after a sale?

Yes. When a sale is completed, QuickPOS deducts the sold quantities from recorded stock. Staff can also record stock-in, stock-out, and correction adjustments with reasons.

Can store owners export QuickPOS reports?

Yes. QuickPOS supports downloadable business records and store statements. Depending on the report, owners can use CSV, PDF, Excel, print, or email delivery options.

Does QuickPOS process card or transfer payments at the counter?

QuickPOS records cash, card, and transfer as sale payment methods so the store has a clear order trail. It does not claim to replace a bank card terminal or the store's transfer account.
