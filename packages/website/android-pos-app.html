Installing the app

Get QuickPOS on Android in three steps.

01

Create and activate your store

Create the owner account on quickpos.com.ng and complete activation for your store.

02

Download the official app

Open the Downloads section of your account portal on the Android device and download the signed QuickPOS app. Android may ask you to allow installation from your browser.

03

Sign in and sync

Open QuickPOS, sign in with your store account, and let it finish the first sync before you start selling.

Frequently asked questions

Android POS app questions answered.

Is QuickPOS available for Android?

Yes. QuickPOS provides a signed Android app for phones and tablets running Android 8 or later. The store owner downloads it from the Downloads section of the QuickPOS account portal after activation.

Can I scan barcodes with my phone camera?

Yes. The QuickPOS Android app can scan product barcodes with the device camera. Busier counters can also use a compatible Bluetooth barcode scanner.

Does the Android POS app work offline?

After the app has completed an online sync, supported sales can be recorded during a connection interruption and synchronized when the connection returns. A verified subscription can be used offline for up to seven days.

Can I use an Android tablet and a Windows computer together?

Yes. One QuickPOS store account works across the Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux apps, so staff can sell from different devices while sharing products, stock, and reports.

Is the QuickPOS Android app free?

The app is included with a QuickPOS store subscription. Activation covers the first five months, then the store renews monthly, quarterly, or yearly with all features included.
