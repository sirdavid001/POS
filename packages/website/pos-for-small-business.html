Made for everyday retail

Keep the counter simple while the business becomes more organised.

A useful small-business POS should make each sale faster and leave the owner with better records afterward. QuickPOS connects the tasks that usually become difficult as a store grows: knowing what sold, keeping stock accurate, controlling staff access, and understanding performance.

01

Record sales without slowing the queue

Search products or scan a barcode, adjust the cart, select a customer, record cash, card, or transfer as the payment method, and produce a clear receipt. Completed sales automatically reduce recorded stock.

02

Know what is available to sell

Organise products by name, category, SKU, barcode, price, and cost price. Set low-stock thresholds, record stock movements, keep supplier details, and review the history behind inventory changes.

03

Give staff the access they need

Create staff accounts with admin, manager, or cashier roles. Role-based navigation helps owners keep sensitive settings and management tasks separate from day-to-day checkout work.

04

See the business beyond today

Review revenue, recent orders, top products, and sales trends. Filter reports by period, download records, and prepare store statements as PDF or Excel files for clearer owner reporting.

Frequently asked questions

What small-business owners ask before starting.

What can a small business do with QuickPOS?

QuickPOS lets a store record sales, manage products and stock, keep customer records, control staff access, print receipts, and review or export sales reports from one system.

Can QuickPOS work when the internet connection drops?

The installed QuickPOS app can queue supported offline sales and sync them when the connection returns. A previously verified subscription may be used offline for up to seven days, but not beyond its known paid expiry date.

Does every staff member need a separate QuickPOS subscription?

No. One active store subscription covers the staff accounts and supported devices used by that store. Owners can assign admin, manager, or cashier roles.

Which devices support QuickPOS?

QuickPOS provides installed app builds for Windows, Android, macOS, and Linux. Store owners access the appropriate official installer through the protected account portal after activation.

How does QuickPOS pricing work?

A new store starts with one activation payment that includes five months of full access. After that, the owner can choose a monthly, quarterly, or yearly renewal plan. Customers in Nigeria pay in NGN, while customers outside Nigeria pay in USD. See the current amounts on the QuickPOS pricing page.
