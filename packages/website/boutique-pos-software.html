Frequently asked questions

Boutique POS questions answered.

Can QuickPOS handle clothing sizes and colours?

Yes, by giving each size or colour its own product record with its own SKU or barcode. QuickPOS does not group sizes under a single parent product, so stock and sales are tracked separately for each size or colour you create.

Can I keep records of my regular customers?

Yes. Save each customer's name, phone, email, address, and notes, keep a loyalty point balance, and select the customer at checkout so their orders stay linked.

Can I give discounts during a sale season?

Yes. Cashiers can apply a discount to an order, and discount totals appear in sales reports and store statements.

Can customers pay by bank transfer?

Yes. QuickPOS records transfer, cash, card, or mobile payment as the payment method on each order. The transfer itself is made through your bank or payment provider.

Can I run my boutique POS on a tablet?

Yes. QuickPOS has an Android app for phones and tablets running Android 8 or later, as well as installed apps for Windows, macOS, and Linux.
